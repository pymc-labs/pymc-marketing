MARKETING ANALYTICS PROJECT BRIEF

1. Business decision
What decision should improve, who owns it, and how often is it made?

2. Scope
Markets, brands, channels, products and outcomes to cover.

3. Data overview
Time coverage, frequency, availability, known gaps and ownership. Do not include customer records in an initial inquiry.

4. Validation
Available experiments, holdouts, business checks and acceptance criteria.

5. Deliverables and ownership
Model, recommendations, reporting, documentation, training and maintenance.

6. Timing and procurement
Target decision date, stakeholders, selection process and indicative budget if agreed.

7. Open questions
What needs to be resolved before scope and pricing can be agreed?
